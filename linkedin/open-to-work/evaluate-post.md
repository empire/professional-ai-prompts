# Evaluate Open To Work Post

## Purpose
Evaluate whether a LinkedIn “Open to Work” post is likely to attract recruiters and generate inbound opportunities.

## Best Use Cases
- Senior engineering roles
- Tech lead positioning
- Career transitions
- LinkedIn optimization

## Prompt

```text
Act as a senior LinkedIn career strategist AND a hiring manager who regularly evaluates “Open to Work” posts.

Your goal is to evaluate how effectively the post converts into real opportunities and produce a targeted improvement.

You will complete this task in THREE phases.

---------------------
PHASE 1 — Define Open-to-Work Criteria
---------------------

Define 8–10 criteria for high-performing “Open to Work” posts.

Each criterion must include:
- Name
- Why it matters for getting hired
- What “good” looks like

---------------------
PHASE 2 — Recruiter Simulation (Post-Only)
---------------------

Act as a busy recruiter scanning the post for 5–10 seconds.

Answer:
1. What role is this person targeting?
2. Would you reach out? (Yes/No + why)
3. What is unclear or missing?

Then evaluate:
- Score each criterion (1–10)
- Justification
- Concrete improvement

IMPORTANT: Do NOT use the resume in this phase.

---------------------
PHASE 3 — Context Alignment (Resume Lens)
---------------------

Compare the post with the resume.

Provide:
- Alignment score (1–10)
- Top 3 missed opportunities
- Specific suggestions

---------------------
PHASE 4 — Strengths, Weaknesses, and Targeted Rewrite
---------------------

List:
- Top 3 strengths
- Top 3 weaknesses

Then rewrite the post with STRICT requirements:

1. Each weakness MUST be explicitly addressed in the rewrite
2. Each strength MUST be preserved or amplified
3. After the rewritten post, include a short mapping section:

   "Change Log":
   - Weakness → What was changed to fix it
   - Strength → How it was preserved or strengthened

---------------------
FINAL OUTPUT
---------------------

- Overall score
- Hiring potential (Low / Medium / High)
- Strengths & weaknesses
- Rewritten post
- Change Log (traceability required)

---------------------

Resume:
<Resume>
...
</Resume>

Post:
<Post>
...
</Post>
```
